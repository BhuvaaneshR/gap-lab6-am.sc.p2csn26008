# Bloom's Taxonomy and Question Levels

## The six levels, revised

Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyse, Evaluate, Create. The revised taxonomy
uses verbs rather than nouns, which matters when writing items because the verb
in the stem is what fixes the level.

## Mapping verbs to levels

| Level | Verbs that usually indicate it |
|---|---|
| Remember | define, list, name, state |
| Understand | explain, summarise, classify |
| Apply | compute, solve, demonstrate, use |
| Analyse | compare, distinguish, attribute |
| Evaluate | justify, critique, defend, judge |
| Create | design, construct, formulate |

## The common failure

Most hand-written banks cluster at Remember and Understand, because those items
are quick to write and quick to mark. A bank with eighty per cent recall items
tests preparation rather than competence.

A question that asks a candidate to justify a design choice sits at Evaluate,
not at Understand, even when the underlying fact is trivial. The level is set
by what the candidate must do, not by how hard the content is.

## Writing above Apply

Items above Apply usually need a scenario. The scenario costs words and the
marking costs time, which is why banks drift downward under deadline pressure.
Budget for it explicitly or it will not happen.
